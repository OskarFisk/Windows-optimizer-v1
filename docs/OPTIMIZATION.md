# Optimization Profiles

## Gaming
Focus on reducing unnecessary background activity without disabling security or essential Windows services. Include a preview of startup/background changes, temporary-file cleanup, power-plan information and game-process resource monitoring.

## Balanced
Keep normal Windows behavior while cleaning temporary data and highlighting startup/background programs that consume resources.

## Laptop / Battery
Prefer Windows-supported power and background-app controls. Avoid permanent aggressive performance settings that increase heat, fan noise or battery drain.

## Restore
Before system-level changes, offer a restore-point step and maintain an internal action log. If Windows cannot create a restore point, explain that clearly instead of pretending protection exists.

## Verification
After applying a profile, re-check services/settings where possible and report what actually changed. Do not promise a fixed percentage FPS or boot-time improvement.
